Pan-malignant enhancer candidate report — GBM atlas 2026-10-07

Source: 8 cohorts, 51 patients, 1,523,668 nuclei. Pan-malignant pool = cells with either CNV-based malignant call (malignant_cnv == 1) OR cell_type == "malignant_unresolved" label. Includes 438,994 CNV-malignant cells from GBM-Space that marker-peak scoring had mis-labeled as TME types.

How to read this table

  • peak_id — GRCh38 coordinates (CATLAS 544K peak atlas); ⚠️ flags chr7 (universal CNV-driven in GBM).
  • nearest_gene / GBM tags — closest RefSeq gene by TSS, color-coded for known glioma driver, GSC-lineage TF, Neftel state marker, chromatin remodeler, or TME myeloid marker (flagged suspicious — these cells were likely CNV-malignant mis-labeled).
  • Distance to TSS — signed, kb. Promoter-proximal (|d| < 2 kb) means this is more likely a promoter than a distal enhancer. 2–100 kb = good distal enhancer range. >100 kb = suspicious (far intergenic, may regulate nothing).
  • Cohorts (n/8) — how many of the 8 atlas cohorts had this peak accessible in their malignant cells. ≥4 is the cross-cohort replication bar.
  • Patients detected / Patients ≥10% — how many of the ~33 patients-with-malignant-cells show the peak at all / show it in ≥10% of their malignant cells (the "accessible per patient" bar).
  • Consistency — fraction of detected patients accessible ≥10%. ≥80% is strong, ≥65% is solid.
  • Strength (mean) — mean fraction of malignant cells with the peak open, averaged across patients.
  • Cloning rec — plain-English: ✅ STRONG, 👍 solid, ⚠️ caveats. These are heuristic, not scores.
Chr7 caveat. chr7+ gain is the defining CNV of GBM — 100% of IDH-WT GBMs have it. Peaks on chr7 are inflated by extra DNA copies, not necessarily cell-type-selective enhancers. We flag them with ⚠️ but still show them. For first-pass cloning, prefer non-chr7 candidates (the attached BED file excludes chr7 and shows top 50 clean hits).
